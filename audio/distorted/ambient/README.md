Original 36-second area soundscapes retained as corruption layers. Clean remasters are in audio/remastered/ambient/. During terminal corruption, the game briefly switches to the matching original here.

Each original has its own arrangement and sound sources:

- `the-quiet-road.wav`: warm sustained strings, a light plucked travel motif, birdlike replies, and soft outdoor air.
- `the-ruined-sanctuary.wav`: low organ chords, resonant tolls, and a deep stone-room drone.
- `the-starlit-archive.wav`: bright glassy clusters and sparse high arpeggios with no wind bed.
- `the-ashen-wilds.wav`: broad gusts, low bowed dissonance, and occasional ember crackle.

All files are 16-bit mono WAV at 22.05 kHz.
